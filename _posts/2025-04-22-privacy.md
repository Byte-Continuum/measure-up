# Privacy Policy for MeasureUp Android Application

## Effective Date: August 26, 2025
This privacy policy explains how the MeasureUp Android application (the "App") handles user data.


## Data Collection and Usage

While the App itself does not directly collect or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, or contacts), we use third-party tools to support the App's maintenance and development.


### AdMob (Google)

The App uses the Google AdMob advertising service. AdMob may collect and use certain data to show you relevant advertisements. This data may include:

- Unique Device Identifiers: Such as the Android Advertising ID.
- Device Information: Type of device, operating system version, and mobile carrier.
- Usage Data: Information about how you interact with ads within the App.

This information is collected directly by Google’s SDKs. You can learn more about how Google uses this data by visiting [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).


## Data Storage and Sharing

- Direct Collection: We do not transmit any personal user data off your device to our own servers.
- Third-Party Sharing: Aside from the data collected by AdMob as described above, we do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to outside parties.


## Permissions

To facilitate the display of advertisements and basic functionality, the App may require:

- Internet Access: To download and display ads.
- Network State: To check if a connection is available for ad delivery.


## Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. AdMob may serve non-personalized ads to users if the developer has flagged the app as being directed toward children or if the user is under the age of consent.


## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.


## Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please contact us through the Google Play Store listing.

**By using the MeasureUp Android application, you acknowledge and agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.**
